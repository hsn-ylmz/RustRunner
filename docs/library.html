 Combines the reports of many tools into one HTML page. any html multiqc 1.35 (bioconda)

 Quality report of an alignment: coverage, mapping quality, insert size and base content. bam html, txt qualimap 2.3 (bioconda)
RSeQC infer experiment
RNA-seq QC
 Works out whether an RNA-seq library is stranded, and which way, from where reads fall on genes. bam, bed txt rseqc 5.0.5 (bioconda)
RSeQC read distribution
RNA-seq QC
 Shows how the reads divide between exons, introns and the regions around genes. bam, bed txt rseqc 5.0.5 (bioconda)
deepTools plotFingerprint
Enrichment
 Shows how well a ChIP-seq or ATAC-seq sample is enriched: a good ChIP puts most of its reads into a small part of the genome, an input control spreads them evenly. bam, bai image, tsv deeptools 3.5.6 (bioconda)
NanoPlot
Long-read QC
 Summarises a Nanopore or PacBio run: how many reads, how long and how accurate, with plots. Reads FASTQ, a Dorado BAM or a sequencing summary. fastq, fasta, bam, tsv html, txt nanoplot 1.48.0 (bioconda)
seqkit stats
Sequence statistics
 Counts the sequences in FASTA or FASTQ files and reports their total length, shortest, longest, N50 and average quality, one line per file. fastq, fasta tsv seqkit 2.14.0 (bioconda)

Trimming 6

ToolWhat it doesReadsMakesInstalled as
fastp
Trim and filter reads
 Fast adapter and quality trimming for FASTQ reads. fastq fastq, html, json fastp 1.3.7 (bioconda)
Cutadapt
Trim and filter reads
 Removes adapter sequences and low-quality ends from reads. fastq fastq, txt cutadapt 5.2 (bioconda)
Filtlong
Long-read filtering
 Keeps the best long reads of a Nanopore or PacBio run: drops reads that are too short or too inaccurate, and can keep only as many bases as you need. Writes a compressed FASTQ. fastq fastq filtlong 0.3.1 (bioconda)
chopper
Long-read filtering
 Filters and trims long reads by their average quality and length. Fast, and the usual first step for Nanopore reads. Writes a compressed FASTQ. fastq fastq chopper 0.14.1 (bioconda)
UMI-tools extract
UMI handling
 Moves each read's UMI (unique molecular identifier) into its name and cuts it, and any spacer next to it, out of the sequence. Run it before aligning so duplicates can be removed later. fastq fastq, txt umi_tools 1.1.6 (bioconda)
Remove reads matching a database (bowtie2)
Remove contaminant reads
 Aligns reads to sequences you do not want (rRNA, tRNA, small RNAs, PhiX, a contaminant genome) and keeps the reads that do not match. Matches are counted in the log, not saved. index, fastq fastq, txt bowtie2 2.5.5 (bioconda)

Peak calling and epigenomics 3

ToolWhat it doesReadsMakesInstalled as
MACS3 callpeak
Peak calling
 Finds the places in the genome where a ChIP-seq (or ATAC-seq) sample has more reads than expected, with an optional input control. Makes sharp peaks, summits and a signal track. bam peaks, bed, tsv, bedgraph macs3 3.0.5 (bioconda)
Genrich
Peak calling
 Finds open chromatin in ATAC-seq data (or enriched regions in ChIP-seq). It reads all replicates together and takes the Tn5 cut sites into account. bam, bed peaks, bed genrich 0.6.1 (bioconda)
HOMER annotatePeaks
Peak annotation
 Tells you where each peak lies: in a promoter, an exon, an intron or between genes, and which gene is closest. Uses the genome FASTA and gene annotation you connect. Needs a Genome FASTA and gene annotation. bed, peaks, fasta, gtf tsv, txt homer 5.1 (bioconda)

Signal tracks
 Turns alignments into a signal track (bigWig) that shows how many reads cover each stretch of the genome, scaled so that samples can be compared. bam, bai, bed bigwig deeptools 3.5.6 (bioconda)

Plots
 Draws a heatmap of the signal around your regions, one row per region, with an average profile on top. matrix image deeptools 3.5.6 (bioconda)
riboWaltz report
Ribosome profiling
 Finds where the ribosome sits in each footprint (the P-site offset) and writes one HTML report: lengths, offsets, 3-nt periodicity, start and stop profiles, regions, codon usage, plus TSV tables. bam, gtf, fasta html, tsv ribowaltz 2.0 (bioconda)

Metagenomics 4

ToolWhat it doesReadsMakesInstalled as
Kraken2 build database
Databases
 Builds a Kraken2 database from your own genomes, for example the few species you expect in a sample. Needs a taxonomy that names every genome. fasta, taxonomy database kraken2 2.17.1 (bioconda)
Kraken2
Classification
 Names the organism each read most likely comes from (its taxonomy) by matching it against a database of genomes. Writes a summary table by species and a line for every read. Needs a Kraken2 database. fastq, fasta, database tsv, txt kraken2 2.17.1 (bioconda)
Bracken prepare database
Databases
 Gets a Kraken2 database ready for Bracken. Makes a new folder that links to your database and adds the files Bracken needs for one read length. Your database is not changed. Needs a Kraken2 database. database database bracken 2.6.1 (bioconda)
Bracken
Abundance
 Turns a Kraken2 summary into better abundance estimates. Reads that Kraken2 could only place on a genus are shared out over the species of that genus. Needs a Bracken database. tsv, txt, database tsv, txt bracken 2.6.1 (bioconda)
